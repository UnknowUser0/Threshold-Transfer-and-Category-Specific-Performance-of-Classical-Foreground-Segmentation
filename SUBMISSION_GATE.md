## Scientific limits retained in the paper

The primary holdout contains six category-level inferential units. Category-disjoint sequence results do not establish transfer to arbitrary deployment domains. Temporal and resolution follow-ups are post-hoc pilots on three sequences selected after viewing prior results. No modern comparator was run because training exposure and splits could not be reconciled confidently with the evaluation holdout. Acceptance cannot be guaranteed.
