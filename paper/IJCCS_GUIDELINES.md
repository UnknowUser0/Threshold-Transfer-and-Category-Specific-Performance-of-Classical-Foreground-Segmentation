# IJCCS Author-Format Notes

Checked 22 November 2026 against the [official IJCCS submission page](https://journal.ugm.ac.id/ijccs/about/submissions) and the [SINTA journal profile](https://sinta.kemdiktisaintek.go.id/journals/profile/1075).

The official instructions specify:

- English manuscript in Microsoft Word format.
- Single-spaced, single-column text on Letter paper, 10–12 pages.
- 3 cm margins on all sides.
- Times New Roman 11 pt text, 18 pt title, and 11 pt italic abstracts.
- Indonesian and English abstracts, each no longer than 200 words, followed by keywords.
- At least 10 references, with 80% primary sources; references in IEEE numeric order and URLs provided where available.
- Author names, email addresses, affiliations, and postal addresses below the title.

The current Word file is generated from the Markdown source with these page and font settings. Page count cannot be certified until rendered in Microsoft Word. The official legacy template is included beside the manuscript. Verify requirements again immediately before submission.
